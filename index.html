Health Policy & Economics | Expert in Value-Based Payment Strategy

I am a health services researcher and economist investigating the most pressing challenges in U.S. healthcare: the consequences of vertical integration between insurers and providers, and how payment models drive changes in healthcare delivery, quality and spending.

The Dual Lens Advantage

What distinguishes my research is the integration of rigorous causal inference with nearly a decade of executive leadership experience.

Academic Rigor

PhD in Health Services Research (Health Economics) from Brown University. Expert in quasi-experimental design, difference-in-differences, and machine learning methods to estimate causal effects in complex markets.

Operational Insight

Nearly 10 years of senior leadership experience at industry-leading ACOs and health insurers. I possess rare operational insight into the strategic incentives that drive consolidation, risk coding, and payment policy.

Core Research Agenda

  • Market Consolidation: Providing the first empirical evidence on how the vertical integration of payer-provider healthcare systems affects prices, steering, quality, and market competition.
  • Alternative Payment Models & Value-Based Payment: Leveraging executive experience negotiating and optimizing value-based contracts (Traditional Medicare, MA, Commercial, and Managed Medicaid) to empirically evaluate how these payment models impact care delivery and patient outcomes.
  • Post-Acute Care & Disparities: Evaluating how payment policy reforms (like IRF/SNF reimbursement) impact access and mortality for vulnerable populations.
